Off the tarmac

And then
there's the dirt.

A purpose-built off-road course inside the same fence - gravel climbs, a concrete bowl, articulation ramps, a measured side-slope, forest trail, and enough loose surface to put a wall of dust behind a car.

A good answer for a group sat in a room since nine. The adrenaline they’ll never forget. Same gate, same catering, same crew.

Safety

Run to
Grand Prix standard.

Safety is top priority. A full trackside LED signalling system, top-grade protection barriers. Grade A homologation - the World Championship standard, in place on an ordinary Tuesday.

After the 2025 Hungarian Grand Prix, the FIM's safety delegate called the circuit's preparations and operations exemplary. Marshal posts, medical centre, extraction and race control are staffed by a top-tier crew trained by the best in the sport.

The region

Meeting day,
holiday week.

Lake Balaton is Hungary’s largest holiday region - beaches and sailing, thermal baths, hillside wineries, and the nightlife of Balatonfüred across the water. Most of it is a ten to twenty minute drive from the gate, and Budapest is still only an hour away.

Partner programmes write themselves, and the delegates who stay the weekend pay for it themselves.

Exhibitions & product days

Stands inside.
Space outside.

Vehicles drive into the hall. Stands, demo rigs and a brand village go up on the apron. Multi-exhibitor expos have run here exactly that way - presentations in the morning, the floor open in the afternoon, everyone under one roof when it rains.
